2. Corpus Case Studies｜语料分析案例

Case 1: Evaluative language in online restaurant reviews

Corpus sample extract: “The service was painfully slow, yet the dessert was absolutely wonderful.”

  • painfully slow: negative appreciation
  • absolutely wonderful: positive appreciation + booster graduation

Case 2: Political news editorial evaluation

Extract: “The bold decision may reshape regional trade patterns.”

  • bold: positive judgement on the actor’s behaviour
  • may: hedging (engagement)

Case 3: Implicit evaluation in academic discussion sections

Extract: Only three participants completed the long‑term follow‑up.

No direct negative adjective, but low completion rate implies criticism of research design (implicit evaluation).
